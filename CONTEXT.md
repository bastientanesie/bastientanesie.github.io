# Personal Site

Bastien Tanésie's personal site: a technical showcase of his skills, made of a blog and a portfolio of past projects. Content and source code are written in English.

## Language

**Post**:
A blog article authored by the site owner, identified by the name of its folder.
_Avoid_: Article, entry

**Project**:
A piece of professional work carried out for a Client, possibly through an Employer, shown in the portfolio with its role, period, Tech tags and Skill tags, and identified by the name of its folder.
_Avoid_: Work, case study, mission

**Credit**:
An acknowledgement of a tool, library, typeface, service, or person the site relies on, grouped into categories.
_Avoid_: Attribution, thanks

**Featured project**:
A Project the site owner has hand-picked to appear on the home page, in addition to its place in the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Highlighted project, pinned project

**Post tag**:
A keyword classifying Posts, with its own listing page. Independent from Project tags.
_Avoid_: Category, label

**Project tag**:
A keyword classifying Projects, with its own listing page. Independent from Post tags. Either a Tech tag or a Skill tag.
_Avoid_: Category, label

**Tech tag**:
A Project tag naming a technology used on the Project (framework, database, language, library).
_Avoid_: Stack tag

**Skill tag**:
A Project tag naming a competency exercised on the Project (architecture, specification, project management).
_Avoid_: Competency tag

**Client**:
The end customer a Project was carried out for.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Employer**:
The company the site owner worked for when carrying out a Project for its Client. Absent when the work was done directly for the Client.
_Avoid_: Company, agency

**Cover**:
The image that illustrates a Post in its header and listings; a Project has a logo instead.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, hero
